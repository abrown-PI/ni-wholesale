NUTRITIONAL INNOVATIONS — PRODUCT GUIDE

NI Product Guide

A quick lookup for the team — what to reach for when.

I put this together so anyone making product recommendations has the info in one place, grouped by what the patient actually needs (gut health, women's health, cardio, etc.) rather than by vitamin vs. mineral.

The formal reference sheet with every label detail is a separate doc — this one is the day-to-day cheat sheet.

Everything below is a private-label NI product, with the original manufacturer's brand name in parens in case a patient already knows it under the other name.

Gut & Digestive

Ultra Spore Biotic — the "starter" daily probiotic

Comparable to MegaSporeBiotic (Microbiome Labs) · 60 caps · ML002

Spore-based, shelf-stable — no fridge required. That makes it the easiest sell for patients who won't stick with a daily fridge routine. 5-strain Bacillus blend (indicus HU36, licheniformis, clausii, subtilis HU58, coagulans), 4 billion CFU per 2 caps.

Start slow: 1 cap every other day for week 1, then 1 daily. Max 2/day.

Ultra Flora — when you need bigger firepower

Comparable to HiFlora 50 (NuMedica) · 60 caps · PINM555

50 billion CFU, 12 strains — Lactobacillus + Bifido + one spore. Reach for this after antibiotics, when a patient's been on a low-count probiotic without noticing anything, or for anyone rebuilding longer-term.

2 caps once daily.

Ultra Pre Powder — the prebiotic to pair with either

Comparable to MegaPre (Microbiome Labs) · 5.1 oz · ML004

Probiotics work better when they have something to eat. This is the fiber blend — Bimuno, PreticX, plus Livaux and ACTAZIN kiwifruit. Mixes into water, juice, or a smoothie.

Half scoop daily for a week, then work up to 1 scoop.

FloraRestore — hand this out WITH the antibiotic script

Comparable to RestorFlora (Microbiome Labs) · 21 caps · ML003

Different play from Ultra Spore — the strains here (Saccharomyces boulardii, B. clausii, B. subtilis HU58, B. coagulans) actually survive alongside antibiotics instead of getting wiped out like Lactobacillus-based probiotics would. That's why the pack is only 21 caps — it's designed for the antibiotic course + a week after.

Week 1: 1 cap 2× daily with food. Then 1 cap daily.

GastroGuard — reflux, bloat, sensitive stomachs

Comparable to MegaGuard (Microbiome Labs) · 60 caps · ML005

Artichoke leaf, deglycyrrhizinated licorice (GutGard), and ginger. Supports normal digestion, keeps H. pylori in check, calms occasional bloating. Reasonable to try before jumping to a PPI.

1 cap 2× daily before meals.

Women's Health

FemFlora Balance — vaginal + UTI support

Comparable to Vaginal Balance (Microbiome Labs) · 60 caps · ML001

Cranberry (500 mg) with a targeted 5-strain Lactobacillus blend — L. crispatus, rhamnosus, gasseri, jensenii, acidophilus. 5.5 billion CFU. Best daily preventive for patients dealing with recurrent yeast or UTIs.

2 caps daily, 18+.

DIM — estrogen metabolism

NuMedica · 120 caps · PINM025

150 mg DIM plus 600 mg curcumin (with BioPerine for absorption). Supports healthy 2-hydroxy / 16-hydroxy estrogen ratios in both men and women — think estrogen-dominance symptoms, PMS, adjunct with hormone therapy.

2 caps once daily.

DHEA 5mg — the lower-dose starting point

NuMedica · 120 caps · PINM331

Lower strength is where most women start, especially over 40, since DHEA drops meaningfully after 30. Prescriber-directed.

Note: not currently listed on the store site — verify the label details when you pull inventory.

DHEA 25mg — higher strength

NuMedica · 90 caps · PINM074

98.5% pure DHEA. Typically used at prescriber direction for hormone support. Not for pregnancy or nursing.

1 cap once daily.

Foundational Daily

Max Multi — the daily multi (no iron)

Comparable to MultiMedica without Iron (NuMedica) · 120 caps · PINM250

Herb-free, iron-free comprehensive multi with Albion TRAACS chelated minerals. Good for men and post-menopausal women. Covers vitamins A/C/D/E/K, activated B's, chelated Ca/Mg/Zn/Se, plus ALA and inositol.

4 caps once daily.

D3K25000 — D3 with K2

Comparable to D3-5000 + K2-200 (NuMedica) · 60 softgels · PINM980

5,000 IU D3 paired with 200 mcg K2 (MK-7). Higher-strength D for documented deficiency or winter months. K2 helps direct the calcium into bone instead of arteries — that pairing is why we carry this combo rather than plain D.

1 softgel daily.

Magnesium Glycinate — the calm/sleep/muscle mag

All-In Nutritionals · 100 caps · MAGGLYCNI

600 mg of the glycinate form — gentle on the stomach, no laxative effect like citrate. This is the one to hand out when someone asks "which magnesium?" — muscle tension, calm, sleep, restless legs, PMS.

B-Balanced — activated B-complex

Comparable to B Replete (NuMedica) · 180 caps · PINM236

Uses 5-MTHF (methylated folate — works in MTHFR patients where standard folic acid doesn't) and the full activated B lineup. Energy, stress response, homocysteine.

1 cap 1–2× daily.

Absorbable Zinc — chelated bisglycinate

NuMedica · 120 caps · PINM148

30 mg TRAACS zinc bisglycinate — absorbs better than picolinate/citrate/gluconate because it stays intact in the gut. Immune, prostate, tissue synthesis.

1 cap daily.

Vit A — retinyl palmitate

NuMedica · 90 caps · PINM586

25,000 IU (7,500 mcg RAE) — a higher-dose A. Vision, tissue, cellular function. Do NOT recommend for pregnancy.

1 cap daily.

E-Balanced — full-spectrum vitamin E

Comparable to Elite E (NuMedica) · 60 softgels · PINM112

Natural tocopherol blend with real gamma content, not just alpha. Most cheap E's are alpha-only — this one gives the broader antioxidant coverage.

1 softgel daily.

Organic Iodine — thyroid drops

Comparable to Thyroxodine (NuMedica) · 2 oz liquid · PINM185

200 mcg iodine per 3 drops. Reach for this when a patient's on thyroid support and might benefit from added iodine for T3/T4 synthesis. Contains alfalfa and kelp.

3 drops in water daily.

Bone & Joint

OsteoPlus — MCHC bone builder

Comparable to OsteoMedica (NuMedica) · 120 caps · PINM061

Microcrystalline hydroxyapatite from grass-fed NZ cattle, cold-processed to keep the collagen and bone growth factors intact. 800 mg calcium plus D3, Mg, phosphorus — a real bone-density formula, not just calcium in a bottle. 30+ years of research on MCHC.

4 caps once daily.

Cardiovascular

EPADHA950 — high-EPA fish oil

Comparable to Omega 950 (NuMedica) · 60 softgels · PINM035

430 mg EPA + 390 mg DHA per softgel. Molecularly distilled anchovy/sardine/mackerel. High enough dose to actually shift triglycerides — good for cardio, joints, brain.

1–3 softgels daily.

Red Yeast Rice — cholesterol support

NuMedica · 90 caps · PINM052

905 mg, extra-strength, citrinin-free. Comes up in statin-alternative conversations — obviously prescriber-directed.

1 cap daily.

Ubiquinol — the active CoQ10

Comparable to CoQ Clear Ubiquinol (NuMedica) · 60 softgels · PINM234

100 mg of ubiquinol (the reduced/active form) — better absorbed than plain ubiquinone, especially in patients over 50. Essential to pair with anyone on a statin since statins deplete CoQ10.

1 softgel 1–2× daily with meals.

Skincare

RECODE GLOW CREAM — peptide firming cream

Iconic Beauty Labs · 1.7 oz · RECODE

Copper peptide (GHK-Cu) and Acetyl Octapeptide-3. Firming/lifting cream with radiance support. Apply AM and PM to clean skin, avoid the eye area.
