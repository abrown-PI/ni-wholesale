How to use this doc

  • Comparable To tells you the original manufacturer product NI's private-label item is based on — useful when a patient asks "is this the same as X?"
  • Key Ingredients lists the active nutrients with doses per serving. Serving size is noted where it isn't 1 capsule/softgel.
  • SKU matches the NI Wholesale catalog for ordering.
  • Nothing here is medical advice — dosing decisions stay with the prescriber.

Probiotics

  • Manufacturer: Microbiome Labs
  • Package Size: 60 Capsules (30 servings)
  • SKU: ML001

What it does. Targeted daily support for vaginal pH, yeast balance, bacterial balance, urinary tract health, plus general immune and digestive support. Combines Lactobacillus strains with cranberry.

Key Ingredients (per 2-capsule serving): - Cranberry Powder (Vaccinium macrocarpon) — 500 mg - Proprietary Probiotic Blend — 126 mg (5.5 Billion CFU) - ASTARTE™ Lactobacillus crispatus (LBV 88®) - Lactobacillus rhamnosus (LBV 96®) - Lactobacillus gasseri (LBV 150N) - Lactobacillus jensenii (LBV 116®) - LA-5® Lactobacillus acidophilus

FloraRestore

  • Comparable To: RestorFlora (Microbiome Labs)
  • Manufacturer: Microbiome Labs
  • Package Size: 21 Capsules
  • SKU: ML003

What it does. A gut-recovery probiotic designed to maintain healthy microbial composition during and after antibiotics or other microbe-disrupting medications. Holds up in the presence of those meds where standard probiotics wouldn't.

Key Ingredients (per capsule): - Probiotic Blend — 370 mg (8 Billion CFU) - Saccharomyces cerevisiae var boulardii (CNCM-I-1079) — 250 mg - Bacillus clausii (SC-109) — 100 mg - Bacillus subtilis HU58™ — 10 mg - Bacillus coagulans (SC208) — 10 mg

Directions: Ages 5+. Week 1: 1 capsule twice daily with food. Week 2 onward: 1 capsule daily with food. Under 5, check with practitioner.
