## Suggested rollout

**Phase 1 — Fill foundation + Zoom asks (Q4 2026)**
Vitamin C, D3/K2 10K IU *(Zoom)*, D3 standalone, B12 sublingual, Melatonin (multi-strength), L-theanine, Ashwagandha, Berkeley Life Nitric Oxide *(Zoom)*, Strengtia *(Zoom)*.
Target: **~10 new SKUs.**

**Phase 2 — Immune + Life-Stage Multis (Q1 2027)**
Elderberry, Quercetin, NAC, Glutathione, Zinc lozenges. Prenatal, Kids multi, Kids D3, Kids probiotic, Senior multi.
Target: **~10 new SKUs.**

**Phase 3 — Specialty categories (Q2 2027)**
Cognitive (Lion's mane, Bacopa, Alpha-GPC), Joint (Glucosamine/Chondroitin/MSM, Turmeric), Metabolic (Berberine, Chromium), Sports (Creatine, Electrolytes). Fill women's/men's hormone gaps.
Target: **~15 new SKUs.**

After all three phases: **~57 total SKUs** — solid mid-catalog for an independent pharmacy supplement rack.
